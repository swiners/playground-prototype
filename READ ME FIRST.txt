PLAYGROUND — SIGN-IN TO ROOM DASHBOARD
A design prototype from the Playground App Modernisation work.


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Most likely you've been sent a LINK. Open it in your browser and you're in —
nothing to download, nothing to install. If we're on a call, share your screen
and we'll follow along from there.

If you were sent a ZIP instead, it runs with no internet at all:

1. Unzip the folder FIRST. Don't open index.html from inside the zip preview —
   Windows and macOS both serve those files from a temporary location and half
   the prototype won't load.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.

Chrome is the one it's been checked in. Safari and Edge should be fine.

Either way nothing installs and nothing phones home — React, the fonts and
every photo travel with it, and there is no server behind it doing anything.


WHAT IT IS, AND WHAT IT ISN'T
-----------------------------
This is a PLAY PROTOTYPE. There is no backend and no database. Every service,
room, child, face, allergy and event in here is invented. The PIN gate is a
string comparison in the browser — theatre, not security. Nothing you do in it
is saved anywhere or sent anywhere.

It is not a build, and it is not a preview of a release date. It's the design
made clickable so we can find out what's wrong with it before anyone builds it.


THE SERVICE YOU'RE LOOKING AT
-----------------------------
Little Bugs Early Learning, with 112 invented children across six rooms: four
single-age rooms — Nursery (1 year), Possum (2), Koala (3) and Kangaroo (4) —
and two mixed-age clubs, Wombat and Bilby, both 3 to 5 on vacation care. The
clubs do no sleep checks and no nappy checks, because that care type doesn't.

Each room is set up around a DIFFERENT situation, deliberately: one is in the
middle of a sleep round, one is busy at the door and over ratio, one is deep
in medication and allergies, one is quiet with nothing due at all. So please
open more than one. A single room is a single situation, not the product.


SIGNING IN
----------
The service username and password arrive pre-filled. The only thing you type
is the 4-digit educator PIN. In the zip it's printed in the test rail (see
below) under Reference. On a link sent for a session the rail is switched off,
so whoever sent you the link has the PIN — just ask.


THE TEST RAIL
-------------
A facilitator panel that lets you jump straight to any situation. Open it with
the small handle on the RIGHT edge of the window, or press Ctrl + . (Cmd + . on
a Mac). Esc closes it.

  Journey       14 numbered scenarios — pick one and the prototype jumps
                straight into that situation, in the room that situation
                belongs to. Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 run the first nine without
                opening the rail. Ctrl+0 resets everything.
  Conditions    online or offline, and whether staffing numbers show on the
                room dashboard.
  Surface       a phone or tablet frame if you'd rather see it at device size
                than filling your whole window, and portrait or landscape.
  Appearance    light and dark.
  Copy link     copies a link that reopens the exact state you're looking at.
                Handy if you find something odd and want to show us.

On a link sent for a customer session the rail is switched off entirely — no
handle, no keyboard shortcut, no way in. If you can't find it, that's
deliberate and not a fault. The panel names the things we're trying to find
out from the session, and reading them first would spoil the answers.


THINGS THAT AREN'T WIRED UP
---------------------------
Deliberate gaps, so you don't spend time reporting them:

  - The camera. It shows a placeholder, not your webcam.
  - The DASHBOARD's date picker popover (the calendar that opens from the date
    pill at the top). Stepping days with the < > arrows does work, and the day
    picker inside a child's profile is fully wired.
  - Four items in the side menu carry a "Not in this prototype" pill:
    Incident records, Learning, Help & support, Account. Everything else in
    that menu works.
  - "Forgot Password?" on the service sign-in screen.

Also not in scope for this piece of work at all, so they're absent rather than
broken: observations and learning stories, messaging families, emergency and
transport lists, checklists, and live staff ratios.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING
-----------------------
The rules baked in here — ratios, attendance, sign-in — are the Australian ones.
If you're reading it with New Zealand in mind, say so and we'll note it, rather
than you feeding back against rules that don't apply.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE SEPTEMBER SESSIONS
-----------------------------------------
Newest first.

Since the last round, the things you'd notice:

  - The counts in the right-hand panel — Sleeping, Still booked in, Signed
    out — are now buttons. Tap "Sleeping 6" and the list narrows to those six;
    tap it again to go back. It's the quickest way to answer "who's asleep?"
    without reading every row. Grouping by Sleep / rest does the same job a
    different way.
  - The quick-log buttons follow whatever the list is showing, and say how
    many children they'll log for — "Sleep check · 6" is the six children in
    front of you, not the whole room. With nothing filtered it still starts
    with everyone signed in, the way it always did.
  - Anything on "Due now" can be logged straight off that list — "Sleep check
    for these 3". Medication is deliberately left out of it: a dose is one
    child at a time, and that one is not meant to be fast.
  - Wombat Club and Bilby Club are mixed-age 3-to-5 vacation care rather than
    school-age, so the ages, the faces and the way they group all match now.

If you were in one of the 9 September sessions, these are the things you
raised, so you can check we heard them right:

  - Times now say am or pm everywhere.
  - Head count is inverted: it lists every child and you tick the ones you can
    actually see. There is no "all present" button, on purpose.
  - The cohort pills are gone. Grouping is a filter bar, and there's a
    "Select all" where the pills used to bulk-select — on the bar in some
    versions of this prototype, on each group heading in others, because
    exactly where it should live is one of the things we're still testing.
    Press and hold a child for half a second to start an ad-hoc selection
    instead.
  - The list is names and what needs doing. The event icons and signed-in times
    are OFF by default now — switch them back on per room in Room settings if
    your room works that way.
  - The top bar is one row on a tablet and two on a phone, so most of what you
    see is the list. On a phone the camera and room settings moved into the
    side menu.
  - The children's faces are AI-generated. They are not real children, on
    purpose: a real child's photo on a tablet that sits in a room all day is a
    consent question, not a picture question.
  - Switching who's recording now asks for that educator's PIN, and there's a
    proper "Log out" next to it.
  - Correcting a record can change what was recorded, not just the time, and
    the reason is now optional.
  - Every pop-up has a visible X in the corner.
  - A medication dose can be recorded even with no authorisation on file; the
    record is flagged instead of blocked.
  - The child profile carries Office's "special circumstances" and "About"
    notes, each contact expands to all their authorisations, and you can open
    any of the last two weeks without leaving the profile.
